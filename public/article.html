Planning a safe two-day hike

A two-day route is the point where planning starts to matter more than fitness. You will carry more water than you think, camp somewhere you have never seen, and make decisions while tired.

Pick a route you can bail out of

Look for at least two exit points that reach a road within a few hours. Mark them on the map before you leave, and tell someone at home which one you would use first.

Water, then shelter, then food

Plan water stops at intervals you could walk even on a slow afternoon. Carry a filter and a backup method. Shelter comes second: check the forecast twice, then pack for the version that is one notch worse.

Pace and turnaround times

Set a turnaround time for the first afternoon and keep it, even if the summit looks close. Arriving at camp with daylight left is the cheapest safety margin there is.

Leave no trace

Camp on durable surfaces, carry out everything you carried in, and keep fires small or skip them. The next walker should not be able to tell you were there.
